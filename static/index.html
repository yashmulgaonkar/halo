Halo Firmware Installer

Plug the T-Display-S3 Long into this computer with USB-C and use Chrome or Edge. If the installer cannot see the board, hold BOOT, tap RST, then release BOOT. Installing one firmware replaces the other, and Wi-Fi is entered again afterward.

HALO ESPHome firmware (old UI)

Home Assistant connects with the ESPHome integration. After install, join the HALO hotspot and enter your Wi-Fi.

HALO LVGL firmware (new UI)

Updated screens on the device. Home Assistant connects with MQTT. After install, join Halo Hotspot and open http://192.168.4.1. Choose erase when the installer asks, so the previous firmware’s partitions are cleared.
